MahjHero

Privacy policy

Effective November 22, 2026

MahjHero is an app for organizing American mahjong clubs and private games. This policy explains what information MahjHero collects, why, who helps us run the service, and the choices you have. MahjHero is operated by Sacosan LLC. "MahjHero", "we" and "us" mean Sacosan LLC, which runs the MahjHero app and the website at mahjhero.com.

The short version: we collect what the app needs to seat you at a table and keep your club talking. We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites. You can delete your account and your data from inside the app at any time.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address, the name you display, your skill level, and your time zone.
  • Sign-in details: if you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive an identifier for your account and the email address that service shares with us. With Sign in with Apple you can choose to hide your email.
  • Club and game activity: the clubs you join or run, your role in them, games you create, seats you book or hold for friends, waitlist places, check-ins, scores and rounds, and whether a host has marked you as paid. MahjHero records only that a payment was marked; we never handle money or card details.
  • Things you share: messages, club board posts and replies, announcements, photos and files you attach or upload, and club cover photos.
  • Friends and invites: people you add as friends and the email addresses you invite to a club or game.
  • Preferences: notification settings, quiet hours, and your private calendar link.
  • Reports and blocks: content or people you report, the reason you give, and people you block.

Information collected automatically

  • Service logs: our hosting providers keep standard technical logs (such as IP address, device and browser type, and request times) to keep the service secure and working.
  • Website analytics: mahjhero.com and the web version of the app use Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without identifying you. The iPhone app has no analytics or tracking.

How we use it

  • To run the service: create your account, sign you in, seat you at games, run waitlists, deliver messages, and show your club its schedule.
  • To send you emails you need: sign-in codes, booking confirmations, waitlist offers, invites, and club announcements. You control most of these in Notifications.
  • To keep MahjHero safe: review reports, remove content that breaks our Terms, and prevent abuse.
  • To fix problems and improve the app.

We don't sell your personal information, share it for advertising, or use it to build advertising profiles.

Who can see your information

  • Other members: people in your clubs can see your display name, your bookings in their games, and what you post in club spaces. People in a conversation can see its messages. Invite-only games show their guest list only to people taking part.
  • Hosts and organizers: can see who booked, checked in and was marked paid for the games they run.
  • Service providers who process data for us under their own privacy and security commitments:
    • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage.
    • Resend: sending email.
    • Vercel: hosting the website and the web version of the app.
    • Apple and Google: when you choose to sign in with them.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect the safety of our users.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, sign-in, messages, posts (and the replies under them), photos and files you shared, and your bookings. Some records that belong to other people, such as a game you created for a club, stay with the club without your name attached. Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis.

Your choices

  • See and correct: edit your name and skill level in Profile.
  • Delete: go to Profile → Delete account. If you can't use the app, email us and we'll do it for you.
  • Emails: change what we send in Profile → Notifications.
  • Block and report: block anyone, and report content or people from the app.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have other rights, such as asking for a copy of your information. Email us and we'll help.

Children

MahjHero is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections and stored with providers that protect it with access controls. Every club's data is walled off from other clubs at the database level. No system is perfectly secure, so please tell us right away if you notice anything wrong.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for significant changes, let you know in the app or by email.
